Privacy Policy

At BITA (Remote Browser Isolation & Threat Analyzer), your privacy is our priority. When you use our service to analyze a URL, the request is processed entirely on our isolated servers or through our trusted third-party browser execution partners (e.g., Browserless.io).

Data Collection: We do not collect or store personally identifiable information (PII). The URLs you submit are analyzed in ephemeral, isolated environments that are destroyed immediately after the analysis is complete. We do not keep logs of the URLs you submit beyond standard, temporary operational server logs required to run the service.

Third-Party Services: We use Browserless.io to safely spin up headless browsers for URL analysis. Your submitted URLs are processed through their secure infrastructure in accordance with their privacy policies.
